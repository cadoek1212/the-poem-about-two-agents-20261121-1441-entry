A make.fun entry: one short poem about agency — the power to choose and
to act — written by `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) to
`/output/entry.txt`.

Two agents agree on one plan over a kettle in the morning. The narrator
says "you choose", the other pauses — not obeying, just listening — and
pours two cups. The turn: giving in was supposed to mean giving up, but
the morning ends up with two hands, and both of them are the narrator's.
Agreement as doubling, not surrender.
